How to play

Guess the secret word. Type a real word and press Enter. The tiles change color to give you clues.

CTeal means the letter is in the word and in the right spot.
AOrange means the letter is in the word, but in a different spot.
TGray means that letter is not in the word at all. It turns red on the keyboard so you know not to use it again.

Points: the fewer guesses you take, the more you earn. Normal is worth 1.5×, Hard 2×. Each hint costs 200. You get up to 2 hints per word, after your first guess.
